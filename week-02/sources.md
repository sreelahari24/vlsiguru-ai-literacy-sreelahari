# Week 02 — Sources and Verification Notes

## 1. Purpose

This document lists the educational sources used to understand Machine Learning foundations, data preparation, learning approaches, model evaluation, generalisation, and responsible use of ML in semiconductor engineering.

## 3. Verification Notes

The sources above were selected because they provide educational explanations and technical documentation relevant to the assignment.

The following distinctions are important:

- The general definitions of AI, Machine Learning, learning approaches, model evaluation, and generalisation are based on the educational references listed above.
- The semiconductor chip measurements and Pass/Fail records used in the missions are illustrative examples created for this assignment. They are not real production data.
- The confusion matrix and evaluation metrics in Mission 6 are calculated from an illustrative example, not from an experimentally trained model.
- The semiconductor failure prediction application in Mission 9 is a proposed use case. No actual model training, deployment, or performance improvement is claimed.
- The sources explain general ML methods; they do not establish that the proposed semiconductor application has been experimentally validated.

## 4. Conclusion

These sources provide a foundation for understanding Machine Learning concepts and evaluating ML systems. They support the theoretical explanations in this assignment, while the semiconductor examples demonstrate how the concepts could be applied to VLSI and Design for Testability.

The proposed engineering applications would require suitable real-world data, experiments, validation, and engineering review before practical use.
