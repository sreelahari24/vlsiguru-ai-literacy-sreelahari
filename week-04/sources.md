# Week 04 – Sources and References

## 1. Google Machine Learning Crash Course

**Website:** https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course/

**Purpose:**

Used as a learning resource to understand machine learning fundamentals, neural networks, training and model evaluation.

## 2. Google – Working with Numerical Data

**Website:** https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course/numerical-data

**Purpose:**

Used to understand numerical inputs, data representation, data cleaning, normalisation and the importance of preparing data before training.

## 3. Google – Datasets, Generalisation and Overfitting

**Website:** https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course/overfitting

**Purpose:**

Used to understand training, validation and test datasets, overfitting and generalisation.

## 4. Google – Neural Networks

**Website:** https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course/neural-networks

**Purpose:**

Used to understand neurons, hidden layers, activation functions, predictions and the basic concepts behind neural network training.

## 5. TensorFlow Neural Network Playground

**Website:** https://playground.tensorflow.org/

**Purpose:**

Used to perform the interactive neural network experiment.

Two network configurations were tested using the Circle dataset:

- Experiment A: Two hidden layers with 4 and 2 neurons.
- Experiment B: Three hidden layers with 4, 2 and 2 neurons.

The experiment helped investigate how increasing network complexity affects classification behaviour and displayed loss values.

## 6. Research Paper – Semiconductor Wafer Defect Classification

**Title:** Inspection and Classification of Semiconductor Wafer Surface Defects Using CNN Deep Learning Networks.

**Authors:** Jong-Chih Chien, Ming-Tao Wu and Jiann-Der Lee.

**Published:** 2020, Applied Sciences.

**Website:** https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/10/15/5340

**Purpose:**

Used to investigate how Convolutional Neural Networks can identify and classify different semiconductor wafer surface defects.

## 7. Research Paper – SqueezeNet for Semiconductor Wafer Defect Detection

**Title:** Defect Detection and Classification on Semiconductor Wafers Using Two-Stage Geometric Transformation-Based Data Augmentation and SqueezeNet Lightweight Convolutional Neural Network.

**Published:** 2023, Computers & Industrial Engineering.

**Website:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0360835223005739

**Purpose:**

Used to understand how data augmentation and lightweight CNN architectures can be applied to semiconductor wafer defect classification.

## 8. Summary

The above resources provide information about data preparation, neural network architecture, model training, evaluation and practical engineering applications.

Google's Machine Learning Crash Course helped with the conceptual foundations, TensorFlow Playground provided hands-on experimentation, and published research papers helped connect deep learning concepts to semiconductor manufacturing.
